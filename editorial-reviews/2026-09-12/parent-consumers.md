# Downstream Consumer Review, 12 September 2026

## Scope and Method

This is a contextual import audit, not a second proof ownership assignment. The review inspected the abstract, input contracts, current-result discussion, boundaries and conclusions of sixteen downstream papers selected by Kernel title matching. It compared those uses to the current frozen claim rows and their canonical owners. The full mathematical-language atlas was reviewed separately in atlas.md.

A title match is a search hint, not evidence that a theorem belongs in a manuscript. Four integrated import groups and fourteen not-applicable groups account for thirty-six Kernel result/paper placements. Each decision in parent-consumers.json binds literal manuscript anchors, a normalized TeX hash, exact frozen source hashes and the Kernel-to-curated result map. Not-applicable decisions do not close the underlying research target.
