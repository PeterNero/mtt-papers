## Material Corrections and Clarifications

- Embedded isometric chain/product maps need an adjoint/reducing-range
  condition to intertwine Hodge operators. Cost pullback alone is weaker.
- Weighted semigroup transport bounds must use one specified comparison
  norm; positivity in different metrics is not a common contraction bound.
- A no-count effect and its Kraus amplitude differ by a square root.
  The underlying correct recorder theorem and probability law are retained.
- Quasifree Hadamard states are not generally a convex class. Their convex
  hull can be used without claiming all its elements remain quasifree.
- The atlas's positive metric, closed-range and source-selection premises
  are explicit. Tangent dimension is not unobstructed moduli dimension or
  the number of physical constants. A failed trial point is not a global no-go.
- Physical eta9 integral quotients require the correct dual lattice and
  literal comparison map. A fiber component test alone does not identify
  a global gerbe obstruction. The frozen T70 numerical rank is 196 despite
  an inherited boolean key mentioning 208; its 70/52/12 conclusion survives.
- The action's weak doublet occupies four real directions, not a real
  rank-one alignment block. Reduction needs nonlinear control, not just
  the Schur-complement identity.
- The graph dictionary states vertex signs, external labels, removal of
  vacuum diagrams and the correct dual-space projector relations.
- Closure strain and spinor consumers distinguish real versus complex
  dimensions, metric-specific polar orthogonality and TT-restricted shorting.
- Hull/Program C correctly distinguish degrees of d-c and d d-c, a sampled
  flow's periodic points, and the analytic source data needed for contraction.
- Theta I--V retain the calibrated targets, with correct gap inequalities,
  period versus kinetic-norm normalization, torus/Landau spectral branches,
  and the separate auxiliary product used in the gravity ansatz.
- Source arithmetic and anomaly-degree corrections already established
  upstream are now imported without reopening the retained results.
