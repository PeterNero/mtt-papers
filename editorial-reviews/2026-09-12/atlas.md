## Substantive Clarification

`PAPER.COH.ATLAS_DIAGNOSTICS.01`: the atlas's four diagnostic slogans require
hypotheses. A positive-definite weighted finite compatibility normal operator
has the claimed kernel. Its positive gap controls distance in the declared
norms, not automatically physical stability. A deformation tangent kernel
modulo gauge is not automatically an unobstructed moduli space or a count of
measured parameters. A trial residual is not a global exclusion certificate.
The example `x^2=0` has tangent dimension1 and an isolated actual zero.
This corrects interpretation without changing any accepted source value.
