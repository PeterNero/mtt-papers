# Revision Audit

## Supersedes

`Modal Triplet Theory: From MTT to General Relativity`, version 2.

## Reason

The previous edition described a standard dimensional reduction as a
first-principles derivation even though it assumed a ten-dimensional
Einstein-Hilbert action. It also described `Y4 x B1 x B2 x B3`, with three
three-dimensional factors, as ten-dimensional; omitted the nonlinear
consistent-truncation condition; suppressed scalar and vector zero modes; and
overstated what a harmonic projection, a spectral gap, and the internal
helicity-two carrier establish.

## Resolution

The new edition is retitled `Controlled Coherent Reduction to
Four-Dimensional Einstein Gravity`. It:

- uses a ten-dimensional bundle over `Y4` with compact six-dimensional fiber
  `X6`;
- treats the `1 < 2 < 3` structure as a six-dimensional filtration rather
  than three independent three-manifolds;
- declares the Lorentzian metric, principal symbol, and higher-dimensional
  Einstein-Hilbert sector as realization inputs;
- gives the exact discarded-field equation and a quantitative gapped
  approximate-truncation theorem;
- audits harmonic moduli, Kaluza-Klein vectors, form-field zero modes, gauge
  zero modes, warping, and stabilization;
- restricts Einstein-Hilbert uniqueness to the standard local,
  diffeomorphism-invariant, metric-only, two-derivative hypotheses;
- corrects the treatment of higher-derivative graviton poles; and
- incorporates the exact `Z64` transverse-traceless support certificate at
  its actual internal-support tier.

## Retained Result

Given a supplied higher-dimensional Einstein-Hilbert sector and a valid
coherent truncation, internal reduction yields a four-dimensional
Einstein-Hilbert term with `kappa4^2 = kappa10^2 / V_W`. Metric variation and
diffeomorphism invariance then give the Einstein equation and covariant
stress-energy conservation in the controlled regime.

## Remaining Boundary

MTT has not yet selected the physical Lorentzian principal symbol and action
from projection alone. The SI Newton/Planck normalization, full conserved
matter response, observed cosmological constant, compactification solution,
and same-source nonlinear gravity theorem remain open. The exact finite
helicity-two support result does not close those gates.
