# Retired Grouped-Index Notice v9 Audit

## Selected archival revision

- Record: `MTT Corpus Navigation: Retired Grouped-Index Notice`
- Superseded artifact: grouped GPT corpus index v8
- Current archival notice: v9
- Publication policy: archive only
- Zenodo policy: publication exempt

## Finding

The grouped index was retrieval infrastructure created for a historical
custom-chat upload limit. It is not a scientific paper, an authority ledger, or
a current status source. Keeping it in the scientific publication queue would
misstate both its purpose and the completeness of the flat paper migration.

## Resolution

Version 9 replaces the grouped index as the canonical entry with a short
retirement and navigation notice. The full v8 source remains adjacent as
`legacy_grouped_index_v8.tex` for provenance. Current papers are stored one per
directory; current status is provided by the MTT Research Kernel; reproducible
calculations are provided by the curated MTT results repository.

The record is a selected current archival revision, but its research upgrade
state remains `retired_reference`. It must not receive a Zenodo deposit and must
not be counted as an unreleased scientific paper.

## Build audit

The notice builds as a two-page PDF without undefined references or overfull
boxes and contains no scientific theorem claims. Both pages were rendered with
Poppler and inspected at full resolution; the retirement rule, current-source
links, and publication-exempt status are legible and unclipped.
