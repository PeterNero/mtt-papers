\begin{abstract}
This notice records the retirement of the former grouped MTT GPT corpus index. The grouping
was created to fit a historical custom-chat file limit and is not a scientific paper,
authority ledger, or current representation of the research corpus. Canonical papers are now
stored individually in the MTT papers repository, while current status, dependencies,
results, blockers, and continuation state are served by the MTT Research Kernel.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Version 9 Revision Note}
\begin{description}
\item[Supersedes.] Version 8 of the grouped GPT corpus index.
\item[Reason.] Topic grouping duplicated paper metadata, became stale as revisions were
released, and obscured the distinction between historical text and current authority.
\item[Resolution.] The grouped source is retained in the adjacent version-8 legacy file;
this canonical notice directs readers and tools to the flat per-paper catalog and live
status model.
\item[Retained content.] The historical source remains available for provenance and for
understanding the earlier custom-chat workflow.
\item[Remaining boundary.] None. This artifact is retired navigation infrastructure and is
not intended for Zenodo publication.
\end{description}

The repository catalog supplies stable paper identifiers, current versions, source and PDF
hashes, revision audits, result references, and Zenodo lineage. The Research Kernel projects
that catalog together with the current A/B authority ledgers, result manifests, blocker
states, repository heads, calculation jobs, and durable handoffs.

\section{Use Rule}

Do not infer present research status from the legacy grouped index. Use the exact current
paper object or A/B row from the Kernel, then follow its source and evidence references.
Corpus search is for discovery; it does not promote an older claim over the current
authority ledger.

\section{Publication Status}

This notice and its historical source remain in the repository so that the migration is
auditable. They are publication-exempt. There is no missing Zenodo deposit associated with
this entry.
