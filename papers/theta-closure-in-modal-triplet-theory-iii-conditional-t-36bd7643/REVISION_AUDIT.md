# Revision Audit: Theta Closure III

Date: 2026-07-29

## Supersedes

This source supersedes the first edition, *Theta Closure in Modal Triplet
Theory III: Twistor-Action Matching and Independent Normalization*.

## Reason

The first edition counted Route B as independent even though it inherited its
metric, harmonic, area, factorization, and coupling bridges from Route A. The
first v2 pass exposed those shared inputs, but still displayed a Hermitian
curvature-norm action as a standard SDYM twistor action and treated the fiber
norm identity as if it followed automatically.

## Resolution

- Replaced the schematic curvature-norm action with the standard holomorphic
  BF-type form used in the SDYM twistor literature.
- Distinguished the Penrose-Ward field correspondence from the additional
  Hermitian reconstruction-and-norm bridge.
- Reclassified the fiber norm and relative coupling identity as an explicit
  assumption rather than a standard reduction theorem.
- Removed the contradictory Cartesian shared-circle factor from the color
  support.
- Added a central picture, reading guide, and numerical worked example.
- Added primary references to Mason and Movshev.

## Retained Result

After the reconstruction-and-norm, direction-sphere, coupling, metric,
harmonic, and color-factorization bridges are declared, the twistor and direct
descriptions return compatible leading overlap formulas.

## Remaining Boundary

The paper does not derive the Hermitian norm bridge from the holomorphic
twistor action, select the auxiliary internal geometry, provide an independent
normalization, control coherence remainders, or identify the auxiliary model
with the q79/Fu-Yau branch.
