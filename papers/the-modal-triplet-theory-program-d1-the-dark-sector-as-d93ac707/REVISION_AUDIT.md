## Context audit

Version 1 was checked against the selected Program C type dictionary, the
current curated Program D1 paper row, the consolidated correction authority,
and primary observational literature for CMB/BAO, weak
lensing/galaxy-clustering, and cluster-collision constraints.

The original paper had one useful interpretive idea: reduced descriptions may
have different encoding-availability profiles, and local descriptive failure
need not be interpreted immediately as a new microscopic ontology. That idea
is retained as a hypothesis.

The original paper nevertheless treated the E9 statistical label as sufficient
for gravitational mass, clustering, scale dependence, and absence of local
interactions. It treated the E8 relational label as sufficient for homogeneity,
negative-pressure behavior, and accelerated expansion. It supplied neither a
covariant stress tensor nor a modified metric equation. The observational
section asserted compatibility without computing a background, perturbations,
lensing response, transfer functions, or likelihood.

The revision therefore replaces the physical identification by a typed
hypothesis, proves its current underdetermination, and states the complete
promotion contract.

## Required corrections and resolutions

### 1. Dark matter was inferred from missing encodings

**Prior claim:** An E9-dominated regime remains gravitationally active,
clusters like massive matter, and lacks non-gravitational interactions.

**Finding:** A statistical encoding fixes neither a stress tensor nor its
equation of state, sound speed, anisotropic stress, or collision term.

**Resolution:** Version 2 proves that E9 does not imply cold, pressureless, or
collisionless behavior. The physical proposal is restated as a conjecture
requiring a selected source law and explicit dark-matter tests.

**Status:** Resolved by corrected theorem and scope.

### 2. Dark energy was inferred from relational encoding

**Prior claim:** E8 relational dominance produces homogeneous accelerated
expansion without a cosmological constant or effective source.

**Finding:** In Einstein gravity the sign of acceleration depends on
`rho + 3p` and any cosmological term; in modified gravity it depends on the
explicit metric equation. E8 determines neither.

**Resolution:** Version 2 proves that E8 alone does not determine acceleration.
The physical proposal is restated as a conjecture requiring a selected
covariant source or modified metric equation.

**Status:** Resolved by corrected theorem and scope.

### 3. No covariant source contract was supplied

**Prior state:** Gravity was called universal bookkeeping, while statistical
or relational data were said to influence curvature.

**Finding:** This language did not specify whether the proposal changed the
source side or the metric side of the field equation.

**Resolution:** Version 2 separates two lawful routes:

- a diffeomorphism-invariant effective action with
  `T_enc = -2/sqrt(-g) delta S_enc/delta g^{-1}`;
- a specified covariant modified metric tensor.

The on-shell conservation theorem is proved for the action route when every
spacetime-dependent nonmetric argument is on shell. If the availability
profile is externally prescribed, the revision requires its Noether response
term to vanish or to appear in an explicit covariant exchange law. Both routes
must state domains, selectors, parameters, conservation, stability, and
observables.

**Status:** Completion contract closed; selected physical source remains open.

### 4. Precision-cosmology compatibility was asserted

**Prior claim:** The interpretation preserved rotation curves, lensing,
large-scale structure, CMB, BAO, and cosmic acceleration at the level of
effective behavior.

**Finding:** No background solution, perturbation equations, transfer
functions, nonlinear execution, covariance treatment, or likelihood was
provided.

**Resolution:** Compatibility claims are withdrawn. Version 2 requires
separate CMB, BAO, supernova, growth, weak-lensing, cluster-collision,
nonlinear-structure, equation-of-state, and held-out gates.

**Status:** Resolved by withdrawal and explicit test contract.

### 5. Falsifiability was overstated

**Prior claim:** A non-gravitational dark-sector detection would falsify the
interpretation, while continued non-detection would support it.

**Finding:** The uncompleted hypothesis has no unique rates or interaction
predictions. Many particle models also permit strongly suppressed direct
signals.

**Resolution:** Version 2 defines model-level falsifiers only after equations,
uncertainties, and a validity domain are fixed. Non-detection is not counted as
a positive prediction of the current paper.

**Status:** Resolved.

### 6. The relation to existing MTT results was ambiguous

**Prior risk:** Fixed points, internal projectors, shared-circle data, or
finite branch results could be read as already supplying the dark-sector
source.

**Finding:** None of those objects is a spacetime stress tensor without a
selected dimensional-reduction/source map.

**Resolution:** Version 2 lists the results that do not close D1 and states
the exact bridge they would need.

**Status:** Resolved.

## Current theorem content

Version 2 proves:

- availability is not ontology;
- availability data alone do not select a source or metric dynamics;
- missing encodings do not themselves source curvature;
- E9 does not imply cold, pressureless, collisionless matter;
- E8 does not imply accelerated expansion;
- a diffeomorphism-invariant effective source action gives covariant
  conservation on shell;
- absence of a forced ontology is weaker than an observational explanation.

## Live frontier preserved

The paper defines a six-tier readiness scale:

- `DS0`: typed availability hypothesis and non-identifiability theorem;
- `DS1`: selected covariant source map or modified metric equation;
- `DS2`: stable homogeneous cosmological solutions;
- `DS3`: linear perturbations and observable transfer functions;
- `DS4`: nonlinear lensing, structure, and cluster-collision execution;
- `DS5`: joint likelihood, uncertainty, provenance, and held-out tests.

Version 2 closes `DS0`. Tiers `DS1-DS5` remain open. No numerical dark-sector
result or selected MTT source is claimed.

guide explains an availability profile as a descriptive mask rather than a
stress-energy source. A deliberately non-selected scalar toy action shows

of what a future source theorem must exclude. Route A and Route B are also
