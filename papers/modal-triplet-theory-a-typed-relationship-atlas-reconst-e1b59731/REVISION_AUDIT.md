## Disposition

The universal Superset Containment Theorem is withdrawn. The paper is
reclassified as a typed relationship atlas. This preserves valid internal MTT
results and conditional cross-framework bridges without using open or withdrawn
downstream claims as premises.

## Withdrawn evidence

The following former claims are not used as established evidence:

- Exact Born-rule emergence from projection.
- Exact Standard Model family/Yukawa closure.
- Unique Hull-Strominger or Fu-Yau vacuum selection.
- Universal Gaussian graviton damping and all-loop finiteness.
- All-order BV/QME, constructive existence, BRST lifting, physical Hilbert
  space, and unitary scattering.
- LQG and asymptotic-safety containment.
- Causal-set derivation from admissibility.
- A shared bottleneck vector controlling all sectors.
- Mock/global fits as evidence of physical closure.
- Fewer free parameters than SM+GR without a complete common-source ledger.

## Replacement vocabulary

| Relationship | Meaning in v3 |
|---|---|
| Embedding | Injective structure-preserving representation of a target model inside one MTT realization |
| Controlled reduction | Reduced dynamics and observables commute with quantified errors on a declared domain |
| Reconstruction | Target-compatible data are supplied to build an MTT realization reproducing the target |
| Conditional bridge | Additional unselected hypotheses connect MTT to a target result |
| Interpretive correspondence | Structural analogy without a state/dynamics/observable map |
| Calibration/postdiction | Target-related data fix parameters or branches and are replayed |
| Held-out prediction | No target information entered construction, calibration, scale choice, uncertainty, or branch selection |

## New formal results

### Containment certificate

A true containment claim must identify the source and target models, typed
maps, domains, dynamics diagram, observable map, errors, symmetries,
locality/positivity/anomaly obligations, validity range, provenance, and
canonicality status.

### Realization underdetermination

If inequivalent MTT realizations produce the same compared observables, target
agreement selects only the corresponding realization fiber. A unique physical
model requires source-independent selection or a canonicality theorem.

### Shared-symbol insufficiency

Using the same parameter name in multiple sectors does not prove one shared
physical primitive. A same-source claim needs one source space and sourced maps
to every sector with common units, normalization, branch, and uncertainty.

## Resulting scope

MTT is described as a unification program with a rigorous internal
spectral/control spine and several conditional bridges. It is not described as
a proved superset of all listed frameworks. Every atlas row can be upgraded by
filling its certificate and advancing through the predictive-closure ladder.
