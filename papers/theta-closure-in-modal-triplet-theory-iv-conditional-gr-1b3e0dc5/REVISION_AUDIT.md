# Revision Audit: Theta Closure IV

Date: 2026-07-29

## Supersedes

This source supersedes the first edition, *Theta Closure in Modal Triplet
Theory IV: Gravity and Cosmology from the Closure Scale*.

## Reason

The first edition promoted a dimensionless auxiliary volume and a gauge
matching scale into numerical gravity and primordial-tensor claims without an
absolute internal length, a higher-dimensional gravity normalization, a
selected physical compactification, or a selected cosmological cutoff.

## Resolution

- Restricted the coefficient `20.0706400 R1^3` to the declared auxiliary
  `S1 x S2 x Nil3` coordinate model.
- Kept the shared circle counted once and explicitly separated this toy product
  from the selected q79/Fu-Yau global branch.
- Restored the physical factor `ell_int^6` and retained `G10` as an
  independent gravity input.
- Defined the reduced Planck-mass convention in the tensor formula.
- Kept the corrected tensor inequality conditional on a separately selected
  cutoff, margin, and cosmological production law.
- Added a dependency diagram and a worked scale-degeneracy example.
- Removed a duplicate informal reference list and added standard
  dimensional-reduction and inflation references.

## Retained Result

The auxiliary product has the stated dimensionless volume coefficient, and
standard unwarped reduction plus a separately assumed cosmological cutoff give
the displayed conditional scaling relations.

## Remaining Boundary

The paper does not identify the auxiliary product with the q79/Fu-Yau
compactification, select the absolute internal scale or `G10`, derive the
reduction from a selected MTT action, select a physical coherence cutoff, or
predict Newton's constant or a primordial tensor amplitude.
