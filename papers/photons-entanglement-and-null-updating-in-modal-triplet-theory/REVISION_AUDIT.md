## Why revision was required

The previous paper inferred null propagation by excluding static and
superluminal updates, inferred photon helicities from informal coherence-cost
language, treated a horizon as loss of a global right inverse, and used the
superseded carrier notation `Y4 x B1 x B2 x B3`. Those statements did not
establish the claimed physical conclusions. Masslessness alone does not select
Maxwell dynamics, noninjective maps may have sections, and global
nonfactorization must be kept distinct from nonlocal propagation.

1. Replaced the carrier by `Y4 x X6` and separated Lorentzian time from compact
   shared-circle phase or holonomy.
2. Defined a typed photon interface record containing the spacetime, upper
   carrier, encoding, Maxwell sector, state, and detector instrument.
3. Proved the null characteristic cone from the Maxwell principal symbol.
4. Proved that the physical transverse quotient modulo gauge has complex
   dimension two and decomposes into helicities `+1` and `-1`.
5. Reclassified "null updating" as an MTT interpretation of transported
   Maxwell characteristics, not an independent speed-of-light derivation.
6. Proved that a nonfactorizing state is compatible with local commutativity
   and no signaling.
7. Treated photon detection as an ordinary local coupling and quantum
   instrument, with disturbance, outcome completion, probability, and record
   stabilization kept distinct.
8. Made lensing and redshift downstream consequences of curved-spacetime
   Maxwell theory and observer-dependent frequency comparison.
9. Replaced global-right-inverse horizon language by a declared code,
   exterior restriction, and code-relative recovery criterion.
10. Reclassified Hawking thermality as an imported curved-spacetime QFT result;
    projection language alone does not derive the KMS state, temperature,
    greybody factors, or information recovery.
11. Distinguished the closed q79 free even-CAR theorem from the still-open
    bosonic Maxwell/CCR source.
12. Added primary external references for massless representations, AQFT
    locality, Maxwell quantization, quantum instruments, entanglement, and
    horizon thermality.
