# Revision Audit: Horizons, Capacity Bounds, and Entropy

## Selected Revision

- Version: v3
- Date: July 2026
- Supersedes: v2.0, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18322069
- Disposition: revise and reclassify as a conditional interface

## Why Revision Was Required

The previous paper conflated five distinct structures:

1. a coherence-capacity certificate;
2. a Lorentzian causal horizon;
3. an entropy functional;
4. a constitutive transport law;
5. a physical recovery channel.

That conflation produced four current correction obligations from the
consolidated corpus reconciliation:

- bounded flux yields an area upper bound, not equality;
- equality requires saturation and an independently derived coefficient;
- recovery must be stated on a code or observable algebra, not as a
  partial right inverse;
- the coefficient \(1/(4G\hbar)\) cannot be inferred from capacity
  terminology.

The full source audit found three further consequences of the same type
error. A capacity imbalance does not create a causal horizon without a
selected evolution and Lorentzian geometry. Capacity loss does not define
entropy. Capacity exhaustion does not prove an arrow of time.

## What Version 3 Proves

Version 3 proves:

- the regular-value condition under which a capacity zero set is a
  codimension-one certificate boundary;
- two counterexamples showing that capacity loss or exhaustion does not
  determine a causal horizon;
- the exact conditional area upper bound
  \[
  \Delta S(\Sigma)\leq
  \kappa\sigma_{\max}\operatorname{Area}(\Sigma);
  \]
- the necessary and sufficient almost-everywhere saturation conditions
  for equality;
- a strictness criterion when either bound is unsaturated on positive
  area;
- a no-go result showing that capacity decrease does not imply entropy
  increase;
- the correct code-relative definition of exterior recovery;
- a typed conditional theorem identifying every additional source needed
  to match black-hole entropy.

## What Version 3 Does Not Claim

The paper does not claim that MTT currently derives:

- a causal black-hole horizon from a capacity zero set;
- Hawking radiation;
- generalized entropy or a Page curve;
- the Bekenstein-Hawking coefficient;
- a universal entropy-capacity comparison;
- saturation of the capacity flux;
- a physical exterior decoder;
- a selected nonperturbative horizon QFT.

These remain separate source obligations.

is used as evidence for a horizon, entropy functional, saturation law, or
