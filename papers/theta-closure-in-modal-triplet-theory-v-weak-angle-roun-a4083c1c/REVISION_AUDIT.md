### Earlier Audit (Retained)

Date: 2026-07-29

## Supersedes

This source supersedes the first edition, *Theta Closure in Modal Triplet
Theory V: Redundant Determination from Gauge Couplings and the Weak Mixing
Angle*.

## Reason

The first edition presented the weak angle as an independent precision test
even though it was reconstructed algebraically from the same measured
hypercharge and weak couplings.

## Resolution

- Derived the exact same-scale, same-scheme weak-angle identity from the
  GUT-normalized overlap convention.
- Classified the selected numerical value as a round trip rather than a
  held-out prediction.
- Explained why an absolute weak-coupling input from the Fermi constant and
  W-boson mass does not supply an independent hypercharge-to-weak ratio.
- Added a central picture, dependency chain, equal-coupling sanity check, and
  explicit uncertainty-propagation calculation.
- Stated four data-separation conditions for a future non-circular test.
- Removed a duplicate informal reference list and added a standard
  electroweak reference.

## Retained Result

For a supplied common-scheme gauge profile, the overlap-ratio formula returns
the corresponding weak mixing angle exactly. The selected profile gives the
stated central value and propagated one-row uncertainty.

## Remaining Boundary

A held-out prediction requires MTT to select the overlap ratio without using
electroweak gauge or weak-angle values, select the absolute normalization and
matching prescription independently, and compare with data excluded from all
selection and calibration steps.
