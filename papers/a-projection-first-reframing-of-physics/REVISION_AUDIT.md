- Version: v2

## Corrections

1. Many-to-one projection is no longer identified with physical
   irreversibility.
2. Lower dynamics requires a fiber-respecting intertwining condition.
3. A right-inverse section is distinguished from recovery of the original
   upper state.
4. Probability, entropy, locality, time, and quantization require additional
   structures.
5. Compact phase is distinguished from Lorentzian time.
6. Bell locality is stated using the corrected microcausality,
   no-signaling, and nonfactorization distinctions.
7. Current MTT examples and open bridges are listed at their actual tiers.
