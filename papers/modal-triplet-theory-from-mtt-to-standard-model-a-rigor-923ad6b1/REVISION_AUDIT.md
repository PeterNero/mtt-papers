## Required corrections and resolutions

### 1. Full-derivation headline

**Prior claim:** MTT derives the full SM from a coherent fixed-point sector.

**Finding:** The selected branch reproduces the renormalized SM at a declared
profile point. Measured renormalized coordinates remain admitted downstream.

**Resolution:** Version 3 states and proves only the released embedded-equivalence
theorem, with the strict no-knob program kept separate.

### 2. Bundle-to-group assignment

**Prior claim:** Three bundles canonically become U(1), SU(2), and SU(3).

**Finding:** The current native carrier theorem is more precise: rank-one circle,
rank-two symplectic, and determinant-trivial rank-three tensors have those
automorphism groups. Their selected chiral representation has a Z6 central
kernel.

**Resolution:** The paper uses the native automorphism theorem and distinguishes
the faithful field-representation quotient from an experimental determination
of all global line operators.

### 3. Family and anomaly structure

**Prior claim:** A Z3 flavor holonomy by itself derives three observed families.

**Finding:** The current exact packet constructs the 48-state family-diagonal
chiral carrier and checks all anomalies. Upstream physical branch selection is
a separate question.

**Resolution:** Version 3 prints the six left-Weyl rows and exact anomaly sums,
and scopes the result to the selected carrier.

### 4. The 27-by-27 matrix

**Prior problem:** The role of the finite matrix was not defined in relation to
the SM field and mass matrices.

**Resolution:** Version 3 derives its dimension as three discrete classes times
the nine-dimensional Hilbert-Schmidt qutrit-Weyl basis, reports the exact packet
checks, and states that it is neither a particle list nor the SM mass matrix.

### 5. Finite Dirac geometry

**Resolution:** Version 3 includes the 96-dimensional finite Hilbert space,

### 6. Higgs sector

**Prior claim:** The usual Higgs sector and EWSB followed from a curvature shift
and RG sign change.

**Finding:** The executed raw finite one-form space has real rank 12 and therefore
contains three scalar doublets. A selected q79/proto-spinor alignment projector

electroweak normalization are not sourced at zero-primitive tier.

**Resolution:** Version 3 reports the actual rank calculation and separates the
one-Higgs projector from absolute action normalization and EWSB value selection.

### 7. Yukawa values and mixing

**Prior claim:** Gaussian overlap examples derive Yukawa hierarchies and CKM/PMNS.

**Finding:** The charged and neutral values are accepted profile rows. CKM is
closed at a prediction-with-uncertainty profile standard, not as a universal

**Resolution:** The toy overlap derivation is removed. A family-intertwiner

### 8. Precision and parameter count

**Finding:** The current result is much stronger computationally: fifteen source

**Resolution:** Version 3 documents the multi-loop workspace and states that one

**Finding:** Strong-CP selection, unique observed-branch selection, and held-out
