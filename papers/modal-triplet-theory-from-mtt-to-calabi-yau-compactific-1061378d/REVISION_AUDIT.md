# Revision Audit: MTT and Calabi-Yau Compactification

## Current revision

- Version: v2
- Date: July 2026
- Supersedes: v1.0, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17071247
- Disposition: conditional realization/embedding; derivation and selection claims withdrawn

## Reason

Version 1 claimed that MTT already contained and selected a Calabi-Yau corner,
that its worldsheet theory and four-dimensional effective theory were
automatically identical to standard compactifications, and that ordinary
Calabi-Yau machinery became predictive functions of MTT parameters.

Those claims exceeded the supplied construction:

1. Vanishing SU(3)-torsion classes were imposed at a parameter locus rather
   than derived from one selected MTT source.
2. Yau uniqueness was treated as uniqueness of a Calabi-Yau geometry, whereas
   it is uniqueness of the Ricci-flat metric within a fixed Kahler class on a
   fixed complex manifold.
3. Holomorphic visible and hidden bundles, stability chambers, HYM
   connections, anomaly representatives and global B-field data were not
   assembled on one carrier.
4. Target-space geometry was promoted to complete worldsheet and
   four-dimensional equivalence without CFT, GSO, modular, normalization or
   reduction-factorization certificates.
5. Compactness of each background was used as though it supplied a uniform
   spectral gap over moduli.
6. Bundle Laplacians over one common base were claimed to commute merely
   because bundle notation used separate tensor factors.
7. A smooth metric deformation was claimed to change Lens-Nil topology into a
   torus/Calabi-Yau topology.
8. The named T6/Z3 worked example omitted the resolution, gauge, twisted-sector
   and spectrum calculations.

## Resolution

Version 2 defines a complete lower Calabi-Yau compactification record and a
typed MTT realization map. It proves a conditional realization theorem: when
one upper state emits every required row and the MTT lower description factors
through the standard compactification functional, the two descriptions agree
on the declared lower data and perturbative order.

The revision also proves:

1. fixed-background spectral-gap and bounded-harmonic-projector admissibility
   for compact elliptic problems; and
2. strong commutation of modal Laplacians on a genuine product manifold with
   an external tensor-product bundle.

Uniform family bounds, generic commutation, worldsheet completion, moduli
stabilization and physical selection are stated as separate obligations.

## Retained content

- Calabi-Yau threefolds as legitimate lower realization targets.
- Yau's Ricci-flat metric theorem with its correct fixed-class scope.
- HYM geometry subject to holomorphy and slope-polystability.
- Standard cohomology, Yukawa, coupling and moduli machinery as conditional
  calculations once a complete compactification record is supplied.
- A flat T6 lower compatibility witness, explicitly classified as
  non-phenomenological and not an MTT source theorem.
- The finite q=79, Cech and rank-two HYM results at their declared tiers.

## Withdrawn content

- A proved or uniquely selected MTT Calabi-Yau corner.
- Automatic (2,2) worldsheet equivalence for arbitrary HYM bundles.
- Automatic four-dimensional EFT identity and moduli stabilization.
- Generic commuting bundle Laplacians.
- Smooth Lens-Nil-to-torus topology deformation.
- The incomplete T6/Z3 worked example.
- Low-energy correlations presented as predictions without selected inputs.

## Relation to the current q=79 program

The q=79 Fu-Yau target is non-Kahler and torsional. It belongs to the
Hull-Strominger branch, not to a strict Ricci-flat Kahler Calabi-Yau branch.
The exact q=79 arithmetic, finite Cech data, finite projected HYM certificate
and rank-two Wiener-contraction result are contextual evidence only for this
paper. They do not construct the Calabi-Yau source record.

## Remaining boundary

A physical MTT-to-Calabi-Yau theorem still needs one selected upper carrier
that emits the topology, complex structure, Kahler class, Ricci-flat metric,
visible and hidden stable bundles, HYM connections, anomaly and global
B-field data, worldsheet completion, reduction factorization and any claimed
moduli-stabilization or selection mechanism.
