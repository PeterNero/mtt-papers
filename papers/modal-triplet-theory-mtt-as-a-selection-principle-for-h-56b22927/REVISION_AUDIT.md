# Revision Audit: MTT and Heterotic Vacuum Selection

## Current revision

- Version: v2
- Date: July 2026
- Supersedes: v1, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17072927

## Reason

Version 1 used "selection" for componentwise constraint satisfaction. Its
fixed-point consistency condition was defined by the compactification
constraints and then declared equivalent to those same constraints. The paper
also inherited an invalid Iwasawa bundle/anomaly/Yukawa chain, treated a
non-integrable Lens-Nil structure as a heterotic compactification, and
overstated a leading-order worldsheet check.

## Resolution

1. Define a five-level selection ladder:
   projected consistency, full equation closure, ansatz-local isolation,
   dynamical attraction, and physical selection.
2. Prove the projected-residual completeness criterion.
3. Prove ansatz-local isolation under an injective quotient derivative while
   excluding global uniqueness without exhaustion.
4. Prove that equations alone determine neither attraction nor basin weights.
5. State a complete finite-ansatz theorem with global-data, residual,
   quotient, and stability hypotheses.
6. Reclassify the Iwasawa case using the audited bundle obstruction.
7. Withdraw the old anomaly, generation, and normalized-Yukawa conclusions.
8. Reclassify Lens-Nil as an auxiliary non-integrable real SU(3)-structure.
9. Retain q=79 arithmetic/branch, finite Cech, and rank-two HYM results only
   at their declared tiers.
10. Replace the old physical-selection claim by an explicit completion
    certificate for the common visible-hidden Fu-Yau tuple, MTT intertwiner,
    attraction domain, and source rule.

future stronger physical-selection claim requires one common rank-three
