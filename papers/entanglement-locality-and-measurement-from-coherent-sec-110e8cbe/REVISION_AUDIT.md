# Revision Audit: Entanglement, Locality, and Measurement v2

## Verdict

The central compatibility claim survives: commuting spacelike observable
algebras can carry nonfactorizing states, so Bell correlations do not require
a superluminal dynamical influence. The MTT-specific statement is
conditional. Locality descends only on the coherent-preserving algebra of a
decomposable projector, and the physical entangled state and detector
instruments must still be selected from one upper source.
