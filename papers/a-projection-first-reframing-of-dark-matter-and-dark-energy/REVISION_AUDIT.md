# Revision Audit: Projection-First Dark-Sector Diagnostics

## Current revision

- Version: v2
- Date: July 2026
- Disposition: falsifiable diagnostic program, not a dark-sector derivation

## Corrections

1. Failed particle encoding is no longer identified with dark matter.
2. Global capacity exhaustion is no longer identified with dark energy.
3. The covariant effective-stress or modified-field-equation requirement is
   explicit.
4. Bianchi consistency and possible exchange currents are stated.
5. Cold, pressureless, clustering, lensing, CMB, and halo requirements are
   separated.
6. Acceleration, equation-of-state, perturbation, and stability requirements
   are separated.
7. Current observations are treated as data to reproduce, not as automatic
   consequences of projection.
8. The current MTT status is recorded as open.

- Projected upper variables can, in principle, generate lower effective
  gravitational sources without being elementary lower particles.
- A common selected metric-response functional could be tested in homogeneous
  and inhomogeneous regimes.
- Constraint load and capacity exhaustion remain useful hypothesis labels.
