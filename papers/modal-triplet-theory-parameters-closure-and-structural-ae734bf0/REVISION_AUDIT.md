## Version 1 claim audit

### MTT derives QM, QFT, spacetime, irreversibility, and cosmology

Decision: withdraw as a global current-status statement.

Reason: the present program contains exact, numerical-certified, profile,
conditional, support, and open results. These cannot be compressed into one
unqualified derivation claim.

Version 2 resolution: the paper discusses only parameter identifiability and
assigns every imported result its current tier.

### All apparent parameters collapse into one invariant admissibility margin

Decision: withdraw.

Reason: the corrected coherence-capacity object is a sourced normalized
reserve vector plus optional metric clearance. Its scalar minimum is
noninjective and cannot reconstruct the rows or their source data.

Version 2 resolution: prove the noninjectivity explicitly and prohibit using
capacity as an automatic source, force, or parameter eliminator.

### MTT has no freely tunable structural parameters

Decision: replace by a tier-relative ledger.

Reason: structural assumptions are not usefully summarized by a scalar
parameter count, while the current profile-standard construction explicitly
uses one shared continuous construction primitive and 15 measured source
coordinates.

Version 2 resolution: define separate columns for assumptions, discrete
choices, construction primitives, measured profile coordinates,
nuisance/metrology inputs, outputs, uncertainties, and data use.

### Persistence of unresolved freedom falsifies MTT

Decision: withdraw.

Reason: an open source theorem is an unfinished research obligation, not a
failed theorem. It becomes a falsifier only after a scoped claim and exit
criterion are declared.

Version 2 resolution: define a falsification capsule with claim, domain,
hypotheses, source, verifier, error budget, and failure ownership.

### Absence of a global right inverse is a theory-level falsifier

Decision: withdraw as a universal criterion.

Reason: consistent quotient maps and nontrivial bundles may have no global
section. The Hopf fibration supplies a standard counterexample.

Version 2 resolution: nonexistence of a section falsifies only a claim whose
contract requires that section on the named domain.

### Few parameters automatically imply many falsifiers

Decision: narrow.

Reason: a small input dimension can increase overconstraint only after one
source is fixed independently and all outputs and errors are declared.

Version 2 resolution: distinguish algebraic, numerical-certificate,
source-provenance, cross-sector, and held-out empirical falsifiers.
