- Paper: *The Modal Triplet Theory Program A1: Coherent Kinematics*

This is a substantive theorem and scope revision. The pregeometric
chart-persistence construction is retained; its former automatic physical
interpretations are not.

## Required corrections

1. **Chart persistence retained: resolved.**
   An encoding trajectory now starts from an actual upper path and a compatible
   interval/chart cover. A chain of nonempty chart overlaps alone is only
   descriptive adjacency.
2. **Physical causality and microcausality separated: resolved.**
   Timelike/null classes and finite propagation require a selected Lorentzian
   metric and hyperbolic physical equation. Operator microcausality separately
   requires a local observable algebra or net and locality-preserving descent.
3. **Horizon language corrected: resolved.**
   The former global-right-inverse argument is removed. The paper distinguishes
   loss of chart continuation, ill-conditioned representative extension,
   failure of exact exterior decoding, breakdown of the physical PDE, and
   geodesic incompleteness.
4. **Encoding termination separated from upper dynamics: resolved.**
   An endpoint of a maximal representation interval terminates one encoding
   contract. It does not imply termination of the upper path or of a regular
   physical worldline.
5. **Position and worldlines made representational: resolved.**
   Position is a declared localization functional of chart support. A worldline
   is an equivalence class of compatible chart representatives generated by an
   upper path.

## Additional repairs found in context

- Added the factor-through criterion required for every exact chart transition.
- Added inverse and cocycle conditions and proved chart-curve gluing.
- Made support and localization domains explicit.
- Required Karcher-mean hypotheses for intrinsic centroids.
- Replaced the claim that contractivity implies continuity with a valid
  Lipschitz composition theorem.
- Removed the inference from chart ordering to a physical causal order.
- Kept stabilization order, physical time, RG scale, and shared-circle phase
  separately typed.
- Defined effective merger only as noninjectivity of an autonomous reduced map.
- Required multivalued, stochastic, or hybrid data for splits.
- Removed automatic selection, probability, and record formation at an
  admissibility boundary.
- Defined the exit set by a named continuous admissibility margin and added a
  separate continuation/reset relation, measure/instrument requirement, and
  record-stability requirement.
- Removed derivations of exclusion, entropy production, and a temporal arrow
  from support coincidence or projection alone.

## Resulting theorem chain

1. Exact chart transitions exist only under the projection--descent
   factor-through criterion.
2. Compatible invertible transitions satisfying the cocycle condition define an
   effective descriptive quotient.
3. An actual upper path represented through these charts defines an encoding
   worldline up to chart-transition equivalence.
4. Explicit Lipschitz hypotheses imply a regular encoding trajectory.
5. A selected hyperbolic physical PDE plus principal-symbol and locality descent
   gives conditional physical support propagation.
6. Encoding boundaries, effective merger, split data, selection instruments,
   records, and physical termination remain separately typed.

## Resulting scope

Program A1 v2 establishes chart-persistence kinematics and a conditional bridge
to physical support propagation. It does not derive a metric, physical time,
null cone, microcausal operator algebra, particle identity, exclusion
principle, physical horizon, outcome, probability, record, entropy law, or
temporal arrow.
