# Finite Microcanonical Shells v1 Revision Audit

## Current version delta

Version 1 supersedes the unversioned paper about classical constraint deltas.
It retains the coarea and approximate-identity mathematics while replacing a
universal projection interpretation by a precise microcanonical measure
theorem.

The revision:

- states the regular-value and finite density-of-states hypotheses;
- normalizes finite shells as ordinary probability measures;
- proves convergence to the coarea microcanonical surface measure;
- supplies an explicit Lipschitz shell-bias estimate;
- separates Hamiltonian invariance from ergodicity and thermalization;
- treats critical energies, noncompact shells, and ensemble equivalence as
  separate problems; and
- states that MTT geometry must independently select the conserved quantity,
  shell profile, and width.

## Retained result

At a regular energy with the stated finite density-of-states hypotheses, a
normalized approximate-identity shell converges to the sharp
microcanonical measure. Energy-profile shells are invariant under
Hamiltonian flow.

## Remaining boundary

No MTT theorem in this paper selects the shell profile or width, proves
ergodicity, resolves general critical levels, or establishes ensemble
equivalence.
