## Disposition of the former central claim

The v1 Projection--Admissibility Obstruction and its claimed chain of
inevitable physical consequences are withdrawn.

The old argument conflated four differently typed objects:

| Object | Correct equation | Actual gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Representative section | `T_t S_t = id_Yt` | Surjectivity set-theoretically; regularity needs a category-specific theorem |
| Exact upper decoder | `D_t T_t = id_A` | Injectivity of `T_t` |
| Autonomous reduced evolution | `F_t P_0 = P_t Phi_t` | Preservation of initial reduction fibers |
| Prior-effective decoder | `E_t F_t = id_Y0` | Injectivity of the descended map `F_t` |

A noninjective surjection can have a representative section. Noninjectivity
therefore cannot support the former global no-section claim. It can prevent
exact upper decoding, while noninjectivity of an already descended effective
map prevents recovery of the prior effective state.

## Replacement theorem chain

The selected v2 source contains the following auditable results.

1. **Typed descent and recovery theorem.** Exact factor-through criterion for
   autonomous effective evolution, exact injectivity gate for upper decoding,
   and the distinct effective-merger obstruction.
2. **Finite-diameter self-map observation.** A strict image-diameter deficit
   proves nonsurjectivity for a typed reduced self-map; it is not transferred
   to a cross-level projection.
3. **Admissibility-vector correction.** A list of declared margins replaces
   the unsupported universal scalar. Any scalar aggregator depends on chosen
   scales and aggregation rules unless a realization proves invariance.
4. **Conditional reduced-kernel theorem.** An upper probability measure and
   disintegration induce a reduced kernel. Autonomous descent makes the
   kernel Dirac; non-Dirac behavior is an additional conditional fact.
5. **Mixing identity.** Invariance and mixing control the projected two-time
   correlation law. They do not select the upper measure, Born weights or a
   full Markov process.
6. **Locality-descent theorem.** Compatible fiberwise compression preserves
   isotony and spacelike commutation.
7. **Fixed-point robustness estimate.** Uniform contraction provides a
   declared basin-local perturbation bound, not unrestricted universality.
