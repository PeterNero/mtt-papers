## Verdict on version 1

The orientation idea remains a useful interpretive hypothesis, but version 1
promoted it beyond the available theorem chain. In particular, the finite
carrier, refinement language, anomaly constraints, a CP phase, and
irreversibility do not by themselves select a cosmological state or a
nonzero baryon abundance.

## New result

Version 2 owns one formal result: the finite carrier non-selection theorem.
For a conjugate pair of nonzero finite sectors with a baryon-sign observable,
the same carrier admits a symmetric state and states with either sign of
asymmetry. Therefore the carrier does not select the state or its abundance.

The theorem is elementary by design. Its role is to block an invalid
promotion and to isolate the actual source problem.

## Completion routes

Version 2 states two non-equivalent lawful routes:

1. derive a selected asymmetric upper state, its branch rule, and its
   state-preserving descent;
2. derive a baryogenesis dynamics with number violation, CP-odd sourcing,
   nonequilibrium transport or a declared alternative, washout control, and a
   numerical prediction.

Neither route is closed.
