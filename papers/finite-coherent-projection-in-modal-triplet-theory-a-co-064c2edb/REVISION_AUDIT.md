# Revision Audit: Finite Coherent Filters in Modal Triplet Theory

## Current revision

- Version: v3
- Date: July 2026
- Disposition: major scoped rewrite

## Contextual decisions

1. The exact operator core is retained and strengthened. Positivity of
   `P chi(A) exp(-tau A) chi(A) P` does not require `[P,A]=0`; commutation is
   required only for the displayed common-eigenbasis formula.
2. A sharp projector, smooth spectral filter, positive effect, POVM, and
   instrument are now distinct typed objects.
3. The Hilbert space, operator, domain, spectrum, spectral window, and filter
   parameter are declared before a theorem is stated.
4. The Foundation's finite q79 Hessian and rank-two Haar projector are
   imported rather than reproved. This paper owns only their filter
   specialization.
5. The new internal-filter UV no-go theorem proves that
   `I_4 tensor B_int` cannot suppress arbitrarily high four-dimensional
   spectral modes.
6. The Euclidean heat kernel remains an exact benchmark, but it is no longer
   inferred from the internal q79 projector.
7. Gauge, gravity, Lorentzian evolution, measurement, and entanglement are
   presented as separate transfer contracts. The earlier universal
   projection-shadow theorem is withdrawn.
8. Circle-Lens-Nil is used as an operator/rank filtration language, not as a
   literal nested physical compactification.

## Retained content

- Positive finite coherent filters.
- Local-kernel and spectral representations of one operator.
- The Gaussian heat kernel and distributional delta limit.
- The interpretive usefulness of finite coherent admissibility.
