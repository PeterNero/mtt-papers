# Revision Audit: A Projection-First Reframing of Information and Computation

## Current revision

- Version: v2
- Date: July 2026
- Disposition: interpretive paper with corrected information and computability boundaries

## Corrections

1. A bare projection is no longer treated as an information channel.
2. Information quantities now require a state or probability law.
3. Classical and quantum data processing are separated.
4. A section is distinguished from state recovery.
5. Noninjectivity is no longer claimed to imply memory, entropy growth, or
   irreversibility.
6. Complexity now requires an encoding, input size, error model, and resource
   measure.
7. Undecidability now requires an unbounded family and an explicit reduction.
8. Fixed finite MTT operators are not advertised as undecidable instances.

## Retained content

- Projection-first language remains useful for identifying which distinctions
  survive an effective description.
- Information is treated as operational distinguishability rather than a new
  physical substance.
- MTT finite projectors and operators remain candidates for quantitative
  channel and recovery calculations.

## Open boundary

- Define selected MTT states and physical instruments for the finite branch.
- Compute distinguishability and recovery certificates.
- Construct a recursive selected MTT family before pursuing undecidability.
- Supply a rigorous reduction from a known undecidable problem.
