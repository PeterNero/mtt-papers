## Corrections

1. The Gaussian FRG shell bound is now an explicit input. The internal q79
   projector does not prove a four-dimensional shell estimate.
2. A Banach-space endpoint is distinguished from a fixed point of an
   autonomous dimensionless flow.
3. Exact conjugacy transports fixed points and spectra. Approximate
   conjugacy produces a residual only.
4. Scheme dependence is bounded by an endpoint-comparison theorem. General
   scheme independence is not claimed.
5. The finite-unstable-subspace theorem now assumes an essential spectral
   radius below one. Compactness of one summand alone is insufficient.
6. A nonlinear unstable manifold is not claimed without smoothness and
   hyperbolicity.
