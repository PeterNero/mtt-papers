## Major Problems in Version 1

1. A bounded projection was said to produce the Polyakov/RNS quantum theory.
   Pullback can produce local couplings only after the worldsheet field
   content, action, normalization, and quantum measure are supplied.
2. A target-space fixed point was identified directly with vanishing beta
   functions and BRST nilpotence. This omitted scheme, order, ghosts,
   criticality, normal ordering, GSO, and exact-CFT requirements.
3. The bosonic dilaton beta-function constant was mixed with the
   ten-dimensional superstring statement.
4. Equality of characteristic numbers on four-cycles was treated as the
   differential Bianchi identity. It does not construct the differential
   representative, flux, or global gerbe.
5. A withdrawn Strominger uniqueness/minimization claim was imported.
6. Modular invariance and ultraviolet finiteness were assumed in the
   Calabi-Yau/toroidal corner without constructing the model's characters,
   GSO sum, measure, or factorization.
7. Momentum-winding symmetry of the circle mass formula was promoted to full
   quantum T-duality.
8. Stabilization, S-duality, holography, and M-theory alignment were asserted
   without source maps or proof.
