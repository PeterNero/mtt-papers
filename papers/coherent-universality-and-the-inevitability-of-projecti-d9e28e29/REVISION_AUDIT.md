## Version 2 claim audit

### All viable theories require a noninvertible projection

Version 2 claim:
Effective measurement irreversibility forces algebraically noninvertible
projection.

Decision:
Withdraw as stated.

Reason:
Operational nonrecoverability depends on the allowed decoder class. A channel
can be invertible as a linear map while its inverse is not completely positive.
A globally unitary dilation can also induce irreversible reduced records.

Version 3 resolution:
Prove only that a genuinely noninjective descent map has no left inverse.
Correct the former left/right-inverse ambiguity and define a recovery defect.

### Stable records require admissible basins with finite margins

Version 2 claim:
Record stability and predictivity force dynamical basins, finite margins, and
threshold or knee behavior.

Decision:
Replace by a conditional stability result.

Reason:
Stable records can arise from attraction, metastability, redundancy,
superselection, or error correction. A finite margin requires topological and
compactness hypotheses. A sharp knee is not automatic.

Version 3 resolution:
Prove positive distance only for compact cores inside open record regions.
Define a separate finite-horizon dynamical retention certificate.

### Predictivity and locality force smooth spectral suppression

Version 2 claim:
Every viable theory must have a UV kernel in a smooth
proper-time/complete-Bernstein/Stieltjes equivalence class.

Decision:
Withdraw.

Reason:
Renormalized continuum QFT, EFT cutoffs, fixed points, modular constructions,
discrete continuum limits, and nonlocal spectral damping are distinct
mechanisms. Low-energy predictivity does not select one.

Version 3 resolution:
Define a UV-control row and prove by an explicit propagator bound that
low-energy agreement does not determine exponential spectral damping.

### Projection and basins inevitably produce discrete causal events

Version 2 claim:
Basin transitions are isolated and countable and therefore recover a
causal-set-like structure.

Decision:
Replace by a regularity theorem.

Reason:
Crossings can accumulate, stochastic paths can revisit boundaries infinitely
often, and causal sets additionally require a partial order and locally finite
order intervals.

Version 3 resolution:
Prove local finiteness for transverse zeros of a C1 event function on a compact
time interval. Keep event law, reset, ordering, and local finiteness as
independent witness fields.

### Admissibility forces Standard-Model-like matter

Version 2 claim:
Anomaly freedom, rational charges, forbidden baryon/lepton violation, and the
near-minimal Standard Model follow from generic projection-induced
admissibility.

Decision:
Withdraw and replace with typed conditional statements.

Reason:
Anomalies depend on dimension, representations, regularization, inflow, and
counterterms. Charge quantization depends on the global gauge group and
normalization. Operator exclusion requires explicit representation and bundle
products. None of these alone selects the observed Standard Model.

Version 3 resolution:
Prove integer weights only for a specified compact U(1), state the total-anomaly
contract including Green--Schwarz inflow, and prove a conditional bundle
obstruction criterion.

### One coherent universality class contains all successful approaches

Version 2 claim:
Collapse models, monitored transitions, asymptotic safety, loop quantum
gravity, causal sets, and noncommutative geometry are partial shadows of one
inevitable class.

Decision:
Withdraw.

Reason:
Shared terminology does not provide transports, common domains, comparison
norms, error bounds, observable maps, or physical equivalence.

Version 3 resolution:
Introduce a seven-axis witness record. Prove that exact classes refine when
axes are added and that approximate pairwise tolerances are not automatically
transitive. Reassess each framework only through its native typed objects.
