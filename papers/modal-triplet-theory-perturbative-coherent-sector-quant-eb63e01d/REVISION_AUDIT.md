# Revision Audit

## Supersedes

`Modal Triplet Theory: Perturbative Coherent-Sector Quantum Gravity and the
Heterotic UV-Completion Boundary`, version 5.

## Reason

Version 5 contained the essential correction to the older Gaussian
ultraviolet argument, but it repeated the same SPT theorem cluster in several
appendices, allowed a declared discrete schedule to read as an MTT-selected
proper-time law, and sometimes described imported perturbative
background-field/BV machinery as though MTT had derived its quantum measure.
It also did not present the complete current twelve-row q79 worldsheet
contract in one place.

## Resolution

The sixth edition:

- assigns the SPT factorization, graph-rank theorem, and spectral no-go to the
  companion Euclidean TT paper;
- states quantum-GR closure only as fixed-order parity conditional on the
  renormalized action, Wilson coefficients, state, gauge fixing, scale, and
  scheme;
- includes Einstein-loop power counting and the nonzero two-loop divergence
  as the all-scale boundary;
- distinguishes exact internal TT support from Lorentzian normalization and
  universal stress response;
- states the q79 fixed-genus heterotic result as a conditional inheritance
  theorem;
- records the authoritative worldsheet contract as five available, two
  partial, and five open rows;
- separates branch orientation from global measure or initial-state
  selection; and
- treats Schwinger-Keldysh double traversal as a contour identity, not a
  theorem that selects time or removes spacetime.
