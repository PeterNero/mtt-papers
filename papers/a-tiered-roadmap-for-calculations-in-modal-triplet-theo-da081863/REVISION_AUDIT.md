> MTT closes embedded renormalized-Standard-Model equivalence at the adopted
> one-shared-physical-primitive/profile standard on the selected branch.

result with imported standard SM quantization, not a zero-knob derivation,
unique observed-branch theorem, or MTT derivation of perturbative
quantization.
