## Why revision was required

Version 1 correctly observed that graph expansions occur in both classical and
quantum theories. It overreached by treating a modal Hessian as a selected physical
propagator, projected graph grammar as automatically the standard Feynman rules, and
coherence as sufficient to explain quantum statistics and physical amplitudes.
