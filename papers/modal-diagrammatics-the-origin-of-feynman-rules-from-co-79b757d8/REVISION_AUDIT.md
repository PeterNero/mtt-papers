## Why revision was required

Version 1 correctly observed that graph expansions occur in both classical and
quantum theories. It overreached by treating a modal Hessian as a selected physical
propagator, projected graph grammar as automatically the standard Feynman rules, and
coherence as sufficient to explain quantum statistics and physical amplitudes.

## Exact changes

1. Replaced the broad origin claim with a finite graded Wick graph-expansion theorem.
2. Added an exact all-retained projection theorem and a closed-sector corollary.
3. Added an identity-intertwining proposition for Ward/BRST/BV descent.
4. Added a nonselection theorem: graph incidence does not determine numerical
   weights, statistics, causal prescription, gauge data, or renormalization.
5. Distinguished algebraic inverses, Euclidean covariances, retarded/advanced Green
   operators, causal propagators, state two-point functions, and Feynman kernels.
6. Assigned theorem ownership correctly:
   - the finite graph and projection theorems are proved here;
   - the selected free q79 even-CAR net is owned by the companion MTT-to-QFT paper;
   - the upper action and nonperturbative interacting gauge-BRST completion remain open.
7. Removed claims that quantumness, quantum gravity, or renormalization follow from
   diagrammatics alone.
8. Added explanatory discussion, examples, a validation checklist, and a current
   status table.
