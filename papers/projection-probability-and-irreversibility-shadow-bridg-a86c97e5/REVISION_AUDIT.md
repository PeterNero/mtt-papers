# Revision Audit: Projection, Probability, and Irreversibility v3

## Verdict

The shadow-bridge idea remains useful, but the prior mathematical spine was invalid:
noninjectivity does not forbid a right inverse of a surjection. Version 3 replaces it
with exact descent and restricted-recovery criteria and separates the three physical
probability laws until a common source is actually constructed.
