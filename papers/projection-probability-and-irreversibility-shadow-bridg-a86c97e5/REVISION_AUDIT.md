# Revision Audit: Projection, Probability, and Irreversibility v3

## Verdict

The shadow-bridge programme remains useful after a substantial correction of its
mathematical spine. Noninjectivity does not forbid a right inverse of a surjection,
does not by itself produce entropy growth, and does not select a physical probability
law. Version 3 therefore separates deterministic descent, recovery on a declared
code, operational probability, and objective history selection.

The current q79 binary one-anchor recorder supplies a genuine positive result that
was not available in the earlier audit: a selected normal state on the commuting
Fock output algebra emits the stopped operational output measure, and
second-moment capture descent is exact on that restricted recorder domain. This is
an operational Born-type result for one selected apparatus. It is not a theorem for
arbitrary apparatus contexts, finite-bandwidth or non-Markov controls, prequantum
probability, or objective selection of one ontic history.
