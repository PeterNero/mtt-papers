# Revision Audit

## Supersedes

`Modal Triplet Theory: From MTT to a UV-Finite, Unitary Quantum Gravity`,
version 4.

## Reason

The released version combined a valid conditional heat-kernel estimate with
invalid physical and graph-theoretic promotions. In particular, it:

- inferred an external proper-time support gap from an internal spectral gap;
- treated positive Laplace representation as sufficient for a
  Stieltjes/Kallen-Lehmann representation;
- claimed that one Gaussian-damped graviton line controlled every ultraviolet
  direction of an arbitrary multiloop graph; and
- promoted those steps to Osterwalder-Schrader positivity, causal support,
  BRST consistency, unitarity, and all-loop ultraviolet finiteness.

## Resolution

The fifth edition is retitled and rebuilt as a conditional Euclidean TT
filter model. It:

- proves the exact SPT factorization only after declaring a proper-time
  support-gap hypothesis;
- gives a compression counterexample showing that an internal gap does not
  imply external Gaussian damping;
- proves the full loop-momentum rank criterion and its equivalent graph-cycle
  formulation;
- supplies a two-loop counterexample to the "one filtered line" claim;
- proves that nonzero positive Stieltjes spectral weight is incompatible with
  permanent Gaussian decay in the same Euclidean spectral variable;
- returns unfiltered matter loops to standard EFT or BPHZ/EG
  renormalization; and
- separates Euclidean convergence from reflection positivity, Lorentzian
  causality, BRST/QME consistency, unitarity, and MTT source selection.

## Retained Result

A declared positive lower endpoint in the proper-time measure yields an exact
heat-kernel factorization and Gaussian Euclidean decay. For any fixed graph,
that decay controls all ultraviolet loop directions exactly when the filtered
line momenta have full rank on loop space, equivalently when the unfiltered
subgraph is a forest.

## Remaining Boundary

Current MTT results establish exact internal TT support on the selected finite
carrier and a separate conditional coherent GR reduction. They do not select
the SPT endpoint from q79 geometry, identify it with a physical Planck scale,
or prove a positive causal Lorentzian filtered theory. The broader physical
QG and heterotic-boundary analysis belongs to the separate coherent-sector
paper.
