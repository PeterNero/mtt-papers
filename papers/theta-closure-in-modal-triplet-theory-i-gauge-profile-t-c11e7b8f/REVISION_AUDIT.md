SMDR v1.3 full-Standard-Model transport to the tadpole-free pure

- `I2/I1 = 0.5110273 +/- 0.0001231`

scale.

force every overlap to one. Version 2 instead fixes period, pointwise, or
cohomological representatives and integrates a weighted gauge-kinetic
coefficient.

phase/holonomy data and is counted once. The circle, effective lens-base, and
nil representatives used here are auxiliary operator supports, not a literal
`S1 x Lens x Nil` compactification. They are not identified with the selected
q79/Fu-Yau candidate.
