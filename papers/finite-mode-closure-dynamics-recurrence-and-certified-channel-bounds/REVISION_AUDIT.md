# Finite-Mode Calculation Companion: Version 1

## Purpose and placement

This is the standalone calculation companion to FP III, not FP VII and not
a replacement for the six foundational papers. It owns the detailed
radial/recurrence and cubic-moment presentation. FP III imports only the
driver distinction and citation. No theorem or proof block from FP III is
duplicated.

## Contextual source review

The four curated records are integrated at snapshot
f141a20ea23c5c3ff19cc2161c0e226e29ade8a7. The written weighted-cutoff,
cubic-heat, parity-heat, stationary-Gibbs and seven-mode boundary notes, plus
the L11 phase algorithm, were read together. The numerical report alone is
not treated as an analytic proof.

- Recurrence: the classical radial chart excludes the second full-line
  polynomial well; flat measure and Dirichlet/Friedrichs quantization are
  supplied. Compact resolvent proves individual norm recurrence, not
  propagator operator-norm recurrence or irreversible mixing.
- Energy: a smooth interior filter avoids the infinite kinetic energy of
  a sharp boundary projection. First-moment tightness is not convergence
  of a finite-matrix dynamics or higher-moment propagation.
- Equilibrium: retain the raw cubic and quartic, the positive radial
  sector-diagonal comparison, the exact independent-angle cubic-square
  moment, all occupations, and the polar trial density. The four displayed
  bounds are rounded upward and use full trace norm, not percentages.
- Channel: declare eta=1/300, momentum (1,1), seven modes, 489 retained
  states, 1,149 outside states, the interior lift, time, incoming/outgoing
  vectors, Gram error and all-pair phase bound. The exact radial dynamics
  has no occupation cutoff. Only three worker norm columns were independently
  replayed, and no L11 mixed-state improvement is claimed.
- Consolidation: retained as historical dependency/scope provenance, not
  an extra theorem or current-source-selection certificate.

## External mathematical context

The functional-analytic framework is standard closed-form, spectral and
min-max analysis; Mehler, Golden-Thompson and quantum Pinsker are cited to
their primary/authoritative sources. The contribution is their source-specific
composition and executed bounds, not invention of those general methods.

## Scientific and release boundary

No measured mass, temperature, cross section, Brownian driver, continuum
limit, selected q79 endpoint or zero-input physical prediction is derived.
Independent analytic review and full worker norm replay remain verification
upgrades. The physical source and limiting-bath problem remain research
targets. No Zenodo operation is authorized.

Artifact validation is recorded in the hash-bound expository review after
the TeX/Markdown/PDF build and visual inspection.
