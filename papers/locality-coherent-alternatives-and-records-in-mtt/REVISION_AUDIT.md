## Contextual Review Decisions

- Measurement is an ordinary physical interaction and record process, not a privileged conscious act.
- The account does not introduce spacelike source shortcuts or measurement dependence.
- It distinguishes no fundamental universal state from no finite laboratory state, no compatible vector, and no common classical assignment over incompatible contexts.
- Coherent alternatives are not equated with actual worlds or with classical ignorance.
- A definite record without automatic global dephasing is marked as an interpretive commitment, not a derived physical theorem.
- The Wigner reversal example explicitly distinguishes a reversible quantum memory from a macroscopic conscious observer and distinguishes retained copies from lost access.
- Bell and Local Friendliness boundaries are retained. Record erasure alone is not claimed to evade their assumptions.
- Closed canonical probability and free-net results are preserved at their actual domains. Older artifact status strings are not allowed to reopen them.
- The 448-atom result is a single-context checkpoint, not a universal noncontextual hidden-variable theory.
- Experimental efficiency and timing are apparatus inputs, not newly predicted fundamental constants.
- Citations identify original external work and existing MTT owners. No duplicated theorem/proof block is introduced.
