# Introduction

The noncommutative geometric (NCG) formulation of the Standard Model provides a remarkably economical geometric description of matter and gravity via the Connes–Chamseddine spectral action. Despite its success, several structural questions remain open within NCG itself: the origin of the finite algebra, fermionic Hilbert space, and Dirac operator; the physical meaning of the cutoff scale and cutoff function; and the apparent freedom of Standard Model parameters.

In this paper we show that these structures are not fundamental but arise as four-dimensional shadows of coherent fixed-point geometry under projection and truncation in Modal Triplet Theory (MTT). This places NCG on the same footing as other effective frameworks—asymptotic safety, loop quantum gravity, and causal set theory—all of which appear as partial encodings of the same coherent sector in companion works.

The present paper completes the matter-sector component of a multi-sector validation program by demonstrating cross-sector closure: the same finite bottleneck vector $`\Theta`$ governs collapse, event structure, ultraviolet completion, canonical geometry, and Standard Model couplings.

# Emergence of the Spectral Triple

<div id="ass:overlap" class="assumption">

**Assumption 4** (Coherent overlap structure). The coherent sector admits finitely many admissible internal overlap channels, stable under truncation and determined by $`\Theta`$.

</div>

<div class="definition">

**Definition 5** (Overlap algebra). Let $`\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{coh}}`$ denote the involutive algebra generated by admissible overlap operators acting on the reduced coherent Hilbert space.

</div>

<div id="prop:finite_algebra" class="proposition">

**Proposition 6** (Finite spectral algebra from coherent overlaps). *Under Assumption <a href="#ass:overlap" data-reference-type="ref" data-reference="ass:overlap">4</a>, the admissible overlap algebra $`\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{coh}}`$ admits a canonical representative (up to Morita equivalence and admissible basis choice) that may be identified with the finite algebra $`\mathcal{A}_F`$ of an almost-commutative spectral triple satisfying unimodularity, KO-dimension, and first-order conditions.*

</div>

<div class="proof">

*Proof.* Admissibility restricts overlaps to finitely many stable channels closed under composition and involution. Imposing unimodularity and KO-dimension constraints selects a canonical real form and representation class. Within this class, $`\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{coh}}`$ admits a representative Morita-equivalent to the standard finite algebra used in the Connes–Chamseddine construction. ◻

</div>

<div class="proposition">

**Proposition 7** (Fermionic Hilbert space). *The fermionic Hilbert space $`\mathcal{H}_F`$ of the spectral triple is the reduced coherent Hilbert space obtained by projecting admissible internal modes.*

</div>

<div id="thm:dirac" class="theorem">

**Theorem 8** (Dirac operator as overlap generator). *Compatibility with the coherent overlap algebra, bounded commutators, grading, and reality conditions selects a canonical Dirac-type operator on the reduced Hilbert space, unique up to bounded perturbations and admissible inner fluctuations. This operator coincides with the Dirac operator of the corresponding almost-commutative spectral triple.*

</div>

<div class="proof">

*Proof.* Coherent overlap dynamics induces a first-order differential generator. Requiring bounded commutators with $`\mathcal{A}_F`$, compatibility with grading and reality inherited from admissibility, and stability under inner fluctuations restricts the generator to a Dirac-type operator. Uniqueness holds up to bounded perturbations and inner fluctuations, as in standard spectral triple theory. ◻

</div>

# The Spectral Action as a Coherent Truncation Shadow

<div id="ass:kernel" class="assumption">

**Assumption 9** (Coherent proper-time kernel). The coherent fixed-point action admits a proper-time representation with a positive measure $`\mu(\tau)`$ supported on $`\tau\ge\tau_0\sim\lambda_\ast^{-1}`$, encoding spectral proper-time (SPT) damping.

</div>

<div id="thm:spectral_shadow" class="theorem">

**Theorem 10** (Spectral action as coherent truncation shadow). *Within the coherent universality class, the spectral action
``` math
S_{\mathrm{spec}} = \mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\, f\!\left(\frac{D^2}{\Lambda^2}\right)
```
arises as the truncation shadow of the coherent fixed-point action. The cutoff scale satisfies $`\Lambda\sim\lambda_\ast^{1/2}`$, and the cutoff function $`f`$ is completely monotone and fixed (up to admissible reparametrization) by the SPT kernel.*

</div>

<div class="proof">

*Proof.* The coherent action admits a proper-time representation
``` math
S_{\mathrm{coh}} = \int_0^\infty \mathrm{d}\tau\, \mu(\tau)\,
\mathop{\mathrm{Tr}}\!\left(e^{-\tau D^2}\right),
```
with $`\mu(\tau)`$ supported on $`\tau\ge\tau_0`$. Truncation replaces the integral by a regulated trace, yielding the spectral action with
``` math
f(x)=\int_{\tau_0}^\infty \mathrm{d}\tau\,\mu(\tau)e^{-\tau x}.
```
Thus $`f`$ belongs to a constrained completely monotone class, and $`\Lambda\sim\tau_0^{-1/2}\sim\lambda_\ast^{1/2}`$. ◻

</div>

# Standard Model Couplings Are Not Independently Tunable

<div class="proposition">

**Proposition 11** (Gauge kinetic normalization). *Gauge kinetic terms arise from overlap normalization and are fixed functions of $`\Theta`$.*

</div>

<div class="proposition">

**Proposition 12** (Yukawa matrices as overlap matrices). *Yukawa matrices encode overlap strengths between coherent internal sectors and are fixed (up to admissible basis changes) by $`\Theta`$.*

</div>

<div id="thm:higgs" class="theorem">

**Theorem 13** (Higgs sector coefficients). *Within a coherent universality class, the Higgs mass parameter and quartic self-coupling appearing in the spectral action are fixed functions of $`\Theta`$ and are not independently tunable.*

</div>

<div class="proof">

*Proof.* The Higgs field arises as an inner fluctuation of $`D_F`$. Its potential is determined by Seeley–DeWitt coefficients, which depend on traces of powers of $`D_F`$. Since $`D_F`$ is fixed (up to admissible fluctuations) by Theorem <a href="#thm:dirac" data-reference-type="ref" data-reference="thm:dirac">8</a>, the Higgs coefficients are fixed by $`\Theta`$. ◻

</div>

# Conclusions

We have shown that the spectral action and the noncommutative geometric formulation of the Standard Model arise as shadows of coherent fixed-point geometry under projection and truncation. Matter, gravity, collapse, spacetime events, ultraviolet completion, and canonical geometry are unified through a single bottleneck vector controlling admissibility.

All results are slab-local and admissibility-conditioned. Together with companion shadow-bridge analyses, this work completes a multi-sector validation program for coherence-based fundamental dynamics.
