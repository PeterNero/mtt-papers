\begin{abstract}
We show that the Connes--Chamseddine spectral action and the associated noncommutative
geometric formulation of the Standard Model arise as four-dimensional shadows of
coherent fixed-point geometry under projection and truncation in Modal Triplet
Theory (MTT). The almost-commutative spectral triple---including its finite algebra,
fermionic Hilbert space, and Dirac operator---emerges as an effective encoding of
coherent-sector overlap geometry. The cutoff scale and cutoff function of the
spectral action are fixed (up to admissible reparametrization) by the coherent
spectral gap and spectral proper-time (SPT) damping, eliminating their apparent
arbitrariness. Gauge couplings, Yukawa matrices, and Higgs-sector parameters are
not independently tunable within a coherent universality class, but are fixed
functions of a single bottleneck vector $\Theta$ that also controls collapse
thresholds, spacetime event density, ultraviolet completion, and canonical geometric
discreteness in independent shadow constructions. All results are slab-local and
admissibility-conditioned.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
% ============================================================

The noncommutative geometric (NCG) formulation of the Standard Model provides a
remarkably economical geometric description of matter and gravity via the
Connes--Chamseddine spectral action. Despite its success, several structural
questions remain open within NCG itself: the origin of the finite algebra,
fermionic Hilbert space, and Dirac operator; the physical meaning of the cutoff
scale and cutoff function; and the apparent freedom of Standard Model parameters.

In this paper we show that these structures are not fundamental but arise as
four-dimensional shadows of coherent fixed-point geometry under projection and
truncation in Modal Triplet Theory (MTT). This places NCG on the same footing as
other effective frameworks---asymptotic safety, loop quantum gravity, and causal
set theory---all of which appear as partial encodings of the same coherent sector
in companion works.

The present paper completes the matter-sector component of a multi-sector
validation program by demonstrating cross-sector closure: the same finite
bottleneck vector $\Theta$ governs collapse, event structure, ultraviolet
completion, canonical geometry, and Standard Model couplings.

\section{Minimal Coherent-Sector Inputs}

\begin{assumption}[Slab-local coherent regime]\label{ass:slab}
All results are asserted on bounded-geometry time slabs where:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(S\arabic*)]
\item the coherent projector is bounded on the controlled domain;
\item a uniform spectral gap $\lambda_\ast$ separates coherent and noncoherent modes;
\item admissible basins possess strictly positive stability margins away from boundaries.
\end{enumerate}
\end{assumption}

\begin{definition}[Bottleneck vector]\label{def:Theta}
The bottleneck vector $\ThetaB$ consists of the finite set of coherent-sector data
controlling projection, truncation, and stability, including the spectral gap,
projector norms, overlap geometry, and symplectic normalization.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
The role of $\ThetaB$ is operational: it is the minimal data required to control
slab-local reduced dynamics. No global claims beyond admissibility are made.
\end{remark}

\section{Emergence of the Spectral Triple}
\label{sec:spectral_triple}
% ============================================================

\begin{assumption}[Coherent overlap structure]\label{ass:overlap}
The coherent sector admits finitely many admissible internal overlap channels,
stable under truncation and determined by $\ThetaB$.
\end{assumption}

\begin{definition}[Overlap algebra]
Let $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{coh}}$ denote the involutive algebra generated by admissible
overlap operators acting on the reduced coherent Hilbert space.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Finite spectral algebra from coherent overlaps]
\label{prop:finite_algebra}
Under Assumption~\ref{ass:overlap}, the admissible overlap algebra
$\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{coh}}$ admits a canonical representative
(up to Morita equivalence and admissible basis choice)
that may be identified with the finite algebra $\mathcal{A}_F$
of an almost-commutative spectral triple satisfying unimodularity,
KO-dimension, and first-order conditions.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Admissibility restricts overlaps to finitely many stable channels closed under
composition and involution. Imposing unimodularity and KO-dimension constraints
selects a canonical real form and representation class. Within this class,
$\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{coh}}$ admits a representative Morita-equivalent to the
standard finite algebra used in the Connes--Chamseddine construction. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Fermionic Hilbert space]
The fermionic Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_F$ of the spectral triple is the reduced
coherent Hilbert space obtained by projecting admissible internal modes.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Dirac operator as overlap generator]
\label{thm:dirac}
Compatibility with the coherent overlap algebra, bounded commutators, grading,
and reality conditions selects a canonical Dirac-type operator on the reduced
Hilbert space, unique up to bounded perturbations and admissible inner
fluctuations. This operator coincides with the Dirac operator of the
corresponding almost-commutative spectral triple.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Coherent overlap dynamics induces a first-order differential generator.
Requiring bounded commutators with $\mathcal{A}_F$, compatibility with grading
and reality inherited from admissibility, and stability under inner fluctuations
restricts the generator to a Dirac-type operator. Uniqueness holds up to bounded
perturbations and inner fluctuations, as in standard spectral triple theory.
\qedhere
\end{proof}

\section{The Spectral Action as a Coherent Truncation Shadow}
% ============================================================

\begin{assumption}[Coherent proper-time kernel]\label{ass:kernel}
The coherent fixed-point action admits a proper-time representation with a positive
measure $\mu(\tau)$ supported on $\tau\ge\tau_0\sim\lambda_\ast^{-1}$, encoding
spectral proper-time (SPT) damping.
\end{assumption}

\begin{theorem}[Spectral action as coherent truncation shadow]
\label{thm:spectral_shadow}
Within the coherent universality class, the spectral action
\[
S_{\mathrm{spec}} = \Tr\, f\!\left(\frac{D^2}{\Lambda^2}\right)
\]
arises as the truncation shadow of the coherent fixed-point action. The cutoff
scale satisfies $\Lambda\sim\lambda_\ast^{1/2}$, and the cutoff function $f$ is
completely monotone and fixed (up to admissible reparametrization) by the SPT
kernel.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The coherent action admits a proper-time representation
\[
S_{\mathrm{coh}} = \int_0^\infty \dd\tau\, \mu(\tau)\,
\Tr\!\left(e^{-\tau D^2}\right),
\]
with $\mu(\tau)$ supported on $\tau\ge\tau_0$. Truncation replaces the integral
by a regulated trace, yielding the spectral action with
\[
f(x)=\int_{\tau_0}^\infty \dd\tau\,\mu(\tau)e^{-\tau x}.
\]
Thus $f$ belongs to a constrained completely monotone class, and
$\Lambda\sim\tau_0^{-1/2}\sim\lambda_\ast^{1/2}$. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\section{Standard Model Couplings Are Not Independently Tunable}
% ============================================================

\begin{proposition}[Gauge kinetic normalization]
Gauge kinetic terms arise from overlap normalization and are fixed functions of
$\ThetaB$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Yukawa matrices as overlap matrices]
Yukawa matrices encode overlap strengths between coherent internal sectors and are
fixed (up to admissible basis changes) by $\ThetaB$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Higgs sector coefficients]
\label{thm:higgs}
Within a coherent universality class, the Higgs mass parameter and quartic
self-coupling appearing in the spectral action are fixed functions of $\ThetaB$ and
are not independently tunable.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Higgs field arises as an inner fluctuation of $D_F$. Its potential is determined
by Seeley--DeWitt coefficients, which depend on traces of powers of $D_F$. Since
$D_F$ is fixed (up to admissible fluctuations) by Theorem~\ref{thm:dirac}, the Higgs
coefficients are fixed by $\ThetaB$. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\section{Conclusions}
% ============================================================

We have shown that the spectral action and the noncommutative geometric formulation
of the Standard Model arise as shadows of coherent fixed-point geometry under
projection and truncation. Matter, gravity, collapse, spacetime events, ultraviolet
completion, and canonical geometry are unified through a single bottleneck vector
controlling admissibility.

All results are slab-local and admissibility-conditioned. Together with companion
shadow-bridge analyses, this work completes a multi-sector validation program for
coherence-based fundamental dynamics.
