## Major Problems in Version 1

1. It described a first-principles MTT derivation while assuming the
   higher-dimensional manifold, action, fields, compact fiber, and reduction
   architecture.
2. It claimed that MTT selected the internal space, radius, and zero-mode
   spectrum without a same-source geometry-and-operator theorem.
3. It treated the constant-time `9 -> 3` description as an independent
   compactification equivalence rather than the spatial slice of one
   supplied `10 -> 4` fibration.
4. It identified three orthogonal three-dimensional modal bundles with
   spacetime directions without a global tangent-bundle construction.
5. It stated that harmonic zero modes were the same as fiberwise constant
   sections. Nonconstant harmonic forms and twisted zero modes invalidate
   that generic identification.
6. It promoted a finite left-invariant coefficient system to equivalence
   with the unrestricted higher-dimensional PDE.
7. It used Nomizu's cohomological theorem as if it proved completeness of
   metrics, connections, field configurations, and nonlinear solutions.
8. It inferred a consistent zero-mode truncation from a scale ratio without
   computing the omitted residual or nonlinear response.
9. It gave gauge-coupling normalizations as universal although they depend
   on the ansatz, generator normalization, scale, and Weyl frame.
10. It treated finite FCC roots as selected vacua and predicted couplings,
    families, thresholds, and masses without selected normalized source
    data.
11. It imported Iwasawa and Lens--Nil examples as physical proof sources,
    although they are not the current q79 Fu--Yau branch.
