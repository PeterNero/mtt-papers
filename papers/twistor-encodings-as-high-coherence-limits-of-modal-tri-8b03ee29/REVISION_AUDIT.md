# Twistor Encoding Paper v2 Release Audit

## Selected revision

- Paper: `Twistor Encodings as a Conditional High-Coherence Corner of MTT`
- Superseded version: v1.0
- Prior release DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.18255155`
- Selected successor: v2
- Controlling correction authority: A10
- Live blocker: B.ACTION.01, upper action and automorphism transfer

## Context audit

Version 1 was checked against the revised Foundation, controlled-truncation,
projection, Theta I, and Theta III papers, and against the standard Penrose,
Ward, nonlinear-graviton, and twistor-action literature.

The useful proposal survives: a selected, approximately autonomous,
four-dimensional self-dual massless sector is a natural candidate for a
twistor encoding. The original paper conflated two independent maps:

1. reduction from upper MTT dynamics to a coherent four-dimensional sector;
2. a twistor correspondence from that sector to holomorphic data.

Version 2 makes their interface explicit.

## Required corrections and resolutions

### 1. Spectral control versus twistor geometry

**Prior claim:** A coherent projector, spectral gap, and small leakage define
the twistor corner.

**Finding:** Those operator data do not supply a four-manifold, conformal
spin structure, twistor distribution, complex leaf space, incidence
relation, or field transform.

**Resolution:** Version 2 proves a finite-dimensional counterexample and
requires conformal-spin and correspondence data as independent source rows.

### 2. Optimality and faithfulness

**Prior claim:** Twistor theory is the optimal and faithful encoding of the
maximally coherent MTT corner.

**Finding:** No comparison class, optimality functional, or injective
reconstruction theorem was supplied.

**Resolution:** The revised paper uses the narrower status
`twistor-compatible`, `controlled encoding`, or `selected descent`, each with
explicit requirements.

### 3. Controlled truncation

**Prior claim:** The Schur-Feshbach correction by itself establishes the
twistor regime.

**Finding:** It controls discarded dynamical modes, not holomorphicity or
self-duality.

**Resolution:** Version 2 derives the exact memory equation and its
gap-controlled norm, while separating reduction, geometry, self-duality, and
reconstruction errors.

### 4. Gap collapse and kernel-valued evolution

**Prior claim:** Gap collapse or divergence of the truncation correction
forces projected dynamics to become kernel-valued.

**Finding:** Failure of an upper bound does not imply failure of
fiber-invariant deterministic descent. The prior proof inferred distinct
outcomes from loss of control without proving they existed.

**Resolution:** Version 2 gives the exact counterexample `L=0`: there is no
positive complementary decay gap, yet the projected evolution is the
single-valued identity.

### 5. Probability and selection

**Prior claim:** Kernel-valued descent signals an MTT selection event and
irreversibility.

**Finding:** A reduced probability kernel additionally needs a source
measure and disintegration; one-history selection needs an instrument,
recorder, stopping law, or other branch source.

**Resolution:** Breakdown of the twistor certificate now means only that the
encoding is uncontrolled on that domain.

### 6. Twistor limitations

**Prior claim:** Twistor theory intrinsically fails for non-self-dual or full
interacting theories.

**Finding:** Twistor actions and amplitude methods extend beyond the simplest
self-dual correspondence, although they require more structure.

**Resolution:** Version 2 treats the self-dual sector as the most transparent
test corner, not the exclusive scope of twistor methods.

### 7. Relation to Theta closure

**Prior claim:** The paper's corner directly supported the Theta action and
normalization route.

**Finding:** Revised Theta III explicitly assumes the twistor corner,
reconstruction map, positive norm, and coupling bridge.

**Resolution:** Version 2 records compatibility but leaves theorem ownership
and conditional normalization calculations in the Theta papers.

## Frontier after revision

The paper-level correctness blocker is resolved. The high-coherence twistor
proposal is now a typed conditional composition theorem with two no-go
results and a ten-row source contract. B.ACTION.01 remains open: one selected
upper MTT action must emit the coherent projector, physical four-dimensional
conformal-spin carrier, self-dual field sector, twistor correspondence,
intertwiner, positive norm, coupling normalization, and error certificate.
