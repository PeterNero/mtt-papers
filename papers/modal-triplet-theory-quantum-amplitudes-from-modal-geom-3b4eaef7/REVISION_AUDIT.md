## Why revision was required

The previous paper treated conditional pAQFT composition, imported standard
gauge quantization, and same-input Standard-Model profile replay as a complete
first-principles amplitude and phenomenology derivation. The current ledger
does not support that promotion.

## Exact changes

1. Defined a complete typed perturbative quantum record containing the local
   algebra, state, free kernels, interaction, BV/BRST data, renormalized
   time-ordered products, parameters, regime, and provenance.
2. Proved a coefficient-transport theorem and LSZ/in-in corollaries.
3. Proved that an action and graph grammar do not select a state or scattering
   regime.
4. Proved a parameter-provenance classification distinguishing replay,
   fitting, and held-out prediction.
5. Separated scattering amplitudes from in-in and local observables.
6. Reclassified dimensional regularization, minimal subtraction, FRG, BRST,
   and LSZ as standard frameworks used under explicit hypotheses rather than
   uniquely derived MTT outputs.
7. Removed unsupported claims of automatic anomaly cancellation, confinement,
   unitarity, and complete phenomenology.
8. Reconciled the paper with the selected free q79 CAR result, the formal
   interacting tier, A04 profile equivalence, and A05 strict-upgrade boundary.
