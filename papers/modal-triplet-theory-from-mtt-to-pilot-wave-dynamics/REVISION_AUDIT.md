## Claim-by-claim audit

### Complete MTT derivation of pilot-wave dynamics

Version 1.0 claim:
MTT derives the wavefunction, Schrodinger equation, Born density, guidance
law, and trajectory without additional variables or ontology.

Decision:
Withdraw globally.

Version 2 resolution:
Define the complete pilot-wave record and prove a conditional reconstruction
theorem. The Hamiltonian, wavefunction, physical configuration, actual
configuration, current, equilibrium provenance, and apparatus are distinct
source rows.

### Literal ten-dimensional tri-product

Version 1.0 claim:
The physical source is the product `Y4 x B1 x B2 x B3`.

Decision:
Retire as canonical geometry.

Version 2 resolution:
Use the current ten-dimensional bundle over a four-dimensional Lorentzian
base with compact six-dimensional fibre. Rank filtrations may organize
operator sectors but are not three independent compact manifolds.

### Hidden variables and ontology

Version 1.0 claim:
No additional hidden variables or ontological assumptions are introduced.

Decision:
Correct.

Reason:
Relative to wavefunction-only quantum mechanics, Bohmian mechanics includes
an actual configuration as additional ontic data.

Version 2 resolution:
Type the actual configuration and its decoder explicitly. MTT may eventually
source this row, but it cannot be omitted rhetorically.

### Continuity and current

Version 1.0 claim:
The Schrodinger continuity equation canonically forces the Bohmian current
and guidance field.

Decision:
Narrow.

Reason:
Divergence-free modifications of the current preserve the same continuity
equation and equivariance.

Version 2 resolution:
Retain the standard scalar current as the target, and require a current
selection or symmetry/composition theorem as an MTT source row.

### Polar Hamilton-Jacobi representation

Version 1.0 claim:
The quantum potential is projection-induced curvature.

Decision:
Reclassify.

Version 2 resolution:
Retain the polar decomposition and quantum-potential identity as a standard
representation of the supplied Schrodinger equation. A geometric
identification requires a separate same-source theorem.

### Equivariance and the Born measure

Version 1.0 claim:
MTT derives the Born measure independently and equivariance is merely a
consistency check.

Decision:
Withdraw globally.

Version 2 resolution:
Equivariance propagates an initially supplied `|psi|^2` ensemble. It does
not source the initial equilibrium distribution. The selected q79 recorder
has an exact operational output measure only on its declared binary domain.

### No global guidance theorem

Version 1.0 claim:
No global guidance law exists because the projection has no measurable
right inverse.

Decision:
Withdraw.

Reason:
A lower velocity field does not require inversion of the upper projection.
Standard Bohmian global-existence theorems cover broad Hamiltonian classes
for almost every equilibrium initial configuration.

Version 2 resolution:
State the actual reconstruction failure modes: loss of wave regularity,
current descent, configuration decoding, maximal-flow existence, coherent
continuation, or instrument scope.

### Measurement

Version 1.0 claim:
Every measurement is an admissibility-barrier crossing and the lower state
must be reinitialized.

Decision:
Withdraw as a general theorem.

Version 2 resolution:
Measurement is an ordinary system-apparatus interaction plus a pointer
record. Standard Bohmian effective collapse uses the actual apparatus
configuration and disjoint pointer packets. An MTT boundary mechanism needs
its own physical source theorem.

### Horizons and cosmology

Version 1.0 claim:
Horizon formation, evaporation, and cosmological selection are
admissibility events at which pilot-wave dynamics necessarily fail.

Decision:
Remove from the nonrelativistic theorem.

Reason:
These claims require separate gravitational, relativistic, and cosmological
models and do not follow from the scalar configuration-space reconstruction.

### Entanglement and locality

Version 1.0 claim:
A global coherent projector explains nonlocal guidance while preserving
relativistic locality.

Decision:
Narrow substantially.

Version 2 resolution:
State explicitly that Bohmian guidance is nonlocal in physical
configuration space. Operational no-signalling holds in quantum equilibrium
under the standard lower theory. An upper-local MTT explanation requires a
descent theorem preserving settings, local algebras, records, independence
assumptions, and marginal statistics.

### Zero-noise stochastic limit

Version 1.0 claim:
Pilot-wave dynamics are the zero-noise peaked-kernel limit of MTT
indivisible stochastic processes.

Decision:
Reclassify as open.

Version 2 resolution:
List the missing selected kernel, scaling, rate functional, unique
minimizer, concentration, and nodal/domain-control obligations.
