- Version: v4

## Reason

The v3 construction mixed valid local Iwasawa geometry with bundle,
stability, anomaly, flux, and Yukawa claims that do not satisfy their global
mathematical prerequisites. It also treated a non-integrable Lens-Nil
SU(3)-structure model as a Hull-Strominger complex compactification.

## Resolution

1. Retain and prove the diagonal Iwasawa balanced structure, torsion, and
   differential `dH`.
2. Prove that the former invariant form `c` is not closed and cannot be first
   Chern data.
3. Withdraw the monad because its line bundles and global Hom-section maps
   were not constructed.
4. Compute the nonzero Maurer-Cartan residual of the printed Dolbeault matrix.
5. Prove the unique signed one-entry repair in the declared ansatz.
6. Prove that the repaired positive-`mu` family is one complex-gauge orbit and
   has a two-dimensional holomorphic commutant, preventing the claimed stable
   bundle interpretation.
7. Enforce the trivial-carrier obstruction `c3 = 0`.
8. Withdraw the old Bianchi coefficient match, generation count, and
   normalized Yukawa value.
9. Reclassify Lens-Nil as an auxiliary real non-integrable SU(3)-structure
   model, not a Hull-Strominger solution.
10. Separate the q79 finite/topological and rank-two HYM results from the open
    physical rank-three Fu-Yau endpoint.
11. Add a typed Hull-Strominger assembly contract and an exact verifier.

## Remaining boundary

The selected q79 program still needs one common physical tuple: a global
Fu-Yau complex threefold, a stable rank-three visible holomorphic bundle with
the required index, compatible hidden and tangent instantons, the differential
Green-Schwarz identity, global gerbe/flux data, and the physical matter
cohomology and overlap map. Separate rank-two or finite results cannot be
silently transferred to that tuple.
