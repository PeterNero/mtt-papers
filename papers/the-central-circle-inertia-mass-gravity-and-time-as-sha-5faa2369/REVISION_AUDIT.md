## Reason

inertia, gravity, physical time, family multiplicity, and irreversible
dynamics. It did not provide the physical operators or readout maps required
for those identifications, and it treated the lack of a right inverse as an
automatic arrow of time.

  intertwiner owned by the corrected Foundation;

  physical HYM connection;

- distinguishes compact phase, stabilization order, Lorentzian time, and RG
  scale;
- replaces right-inverse language by the exact quotient-dynamics criterion;
  and

  family interpretation.
