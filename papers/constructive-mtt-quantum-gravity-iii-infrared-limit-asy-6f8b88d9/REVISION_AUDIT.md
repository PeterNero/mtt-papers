# Constructive MTT Quantum Gravity III v2 Revision Audit

## Overall verdict

Version 1 did not prove a physical MTT gravity scattering theorem. It assumed a
positive TT mass gap, inherited a physical Hilbert space and Borel-summed
gravity theory that the corrected predecessors do not construct, used a
circular notion of asymptotic flatness, and inferred unitarity from isometric
wave operators without proving a range theorem.

Version 2 withdraws that closure claim. It retains standard massive
Haag--Ruelle theory as a conditional benchmark and replaces the physical
gravity conclusion with a seven-object massless infrared contract.

## New exact results

1. A finite-slab obstruction for infinite-time scattering limits.
2. Infrared neutrality of a normalized SPT multiplier:
   `F(p^2)/p^2 = 1/p^2 + O(1)`.
3. The wave-operator range criterion:
   `S = Omega_+^* Omega_-` is unitary exactly when the two isometric ranges
   coincide.
4. A seven-object selected MTT massless-infrared exit contract.
